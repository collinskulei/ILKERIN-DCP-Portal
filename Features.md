## Here is how the streamlined architecture will work from an IT perspective:

1. How It Works (Frictionless for Clients, Powerful for Case Managers)
For Clients (Zoho WorkDrive Links): Clients won't need new logins or portals. We simply share secure Zoho WorkDrive upload folders tailored to their company. They drag and drop their required Stage 1, 2, or 3 documents directly.

For Case Managers (Internal Workflow App): Our custom internal app will integrate directly with Zoho WorkDrive. Every time a client drops a file into WorkDrive, the app automatically reads the folder, updates the task checklist, logs the document, and advances the stage status.

2. Key Internal Features We will Automate
Automated CRB & Document Expiry Tracking: The app will index uploaded documents and flag time-sensitive files (like 3-month CRB clearance reports) before Stage 2 submission.

## Centralized Case Management Dashboard: A single, live view across all active clients showing stage progress, outstanding tasks, missing files, and CBK query logs without manually compiling status reports.

## Automated Reminders: Internal alerts to case managers (and automated email triggers to clients) for missing or overdue documents.
